EVERYDAY NPCs - PSX TOWNSFOLK & ZOMBIES - FREE SAMPLE   (v1.4)
==============================================================
3 low-poly PS1-style characters from Everyday NPCs Vol. 1:
  NPC_OfficeWorker, NPC_Nurse   clips: Idle, Walk, Run, Talk, Wave, Sit
  NPC_Student_Zombie            clips: ZombieIdle, ZombieWalk, ZombieAttack
                                (own mesh: torn clothes, glowing eyes via
                                 NPC_Student_Zombie_emission.png)

GLB (Godot / glTF) and FBX (Unity / Unreal / Blender), 512x512 textures
(256-colour palette): use NEAREST / Point filtering for the crisp PSX look.
All characters share one 17-bone humanoid skeleton (Godot humanoid names).

Unity: put Unity/EverydayNPCsImport.cs in an "Editor" folder, then import
the characters (Humanoid rig, loops and point filtering are set for you).
Keep each .png next to its .fbx. Tested in Unity 6 (URP).

Godot: Godot_PSX_Shader/ has the PS1 shader from the full pack (vertex
wobble, affine textures, low-res + 15-bit dithering).

THE FULL PACK has 22 characters + 22 infected (44 models), 3 outfits per
character, 35 animations, props, a web demo and (Source edition) all .blend
files and a Godot demo project:
  https://sh4rkdev.itch.io/everyday-npcs-psx-modern-town
