HOW THIS WAS MADE
-----------------
Every character in this sampler is AI-generated. The bodies were generated with MakeHuman (MPFB, whose
output is CC0) from parameter values chosen by an AI system, decimated and rigged by scripts that an AI
system wrote. Every texture is an AI painting made over the model's own silhouette and projected back
onto the mesh: the marine sergeant, orc warrior, patrol officer, nutcracker soldier, shambler, construction worker, head nurse, the stranger, scavenger, syndicate boss, British rifleman, fire fiend and black knight by the FLUX.2 klein image model (Cloudflare Workers AI), the other
10 by an OpenAI image model. The skeleton and the animation clips are the CC0 Quaternius Universal
Animation Library (see NOTICE.txt). The storefront listing states the same thing: Code = yes,
Graphics = yes, Sounds = no, Text and Dialog = no.
